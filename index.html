🧟 ZOMBIE NIGHT SHIFT

You are the night guard. Survive 12 nights in the graveyard street — walkers, runners, spitters and brutes, with a boss on Nights 4, 8 and 12. Earn coins, buy weapon upgrades and barricades between nights. Progress saves in your browser.

How to Play
🖱️ Move: WASD / Arrow keys  •  Aim: Mouse  •  Shoot: Click / Space  •  Reload: R  •  Pause: P / Esc  •  Mute: M
📱 Touch: left joystick to move, FIRE button to shoot (aims at nearest zombie).
Kill zombies to earn coins. Spitters shoot green goo — dodge it! Barricades block zombies. Lose all 3 hearts and the night is over.
Select Night (unlocked nights only — progress is saved)
